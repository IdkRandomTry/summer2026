# Content Provenance & Authenticity
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=saqAYgWanwg

- Open-source technical standard attaching cryptographically bound metadata (**Content Credentials**) to digital media. **Purpose:** Acts as a tamper-evident digital "nutrition label" to trace asset history and identify AI-generated content.
Core Mechanics
- **Provenance:** Tracks origin (camera vs. AI model) and an audit trail of subsequent edits (e.g., Photoshop tweaks).
- **Cryptography:** Uses digital signatures to lock the metadata manifest to the media bits. Unauthorized edits break the signature.
### Limitations
- It keeps original image as part of proof, which removes anonymity.
