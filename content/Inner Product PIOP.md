The inner product relation $R_{\text{ip}}$ is defined as:
$$R_{\text{ip}} = \{ ([[f]], g, c); f) : c = \sum_{\boldsymbol{x} \in \mathbb{B}_\mu} f(\boldsymbol{x}) \cdot g(\boldsymbol{x}) \}$$
where $f, g$ are multilinear polynomials with $f, g \in \mathbb{F}[X_1, \dots, X_\mu]$.

Since $g$ is public, this can be done by a [[Sum Check PIOP]] on where an oracle to $f(x)\cdot g(x)$ is used.
