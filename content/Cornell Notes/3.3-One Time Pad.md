The One-Time Pad encryption scheme is described by the following 5-tuple $(\mathcal{M}, \mathcal{K}, \text{Gen}, \text{Enc}, \text{Dec})$:  $$ \begin{aligned} \mathcal{M} &= \{0,1\}^n \\ \mathcal{K} &= \{0,1\}^n \\  \text{Gen} &= k = k_1k_2\dots k_n \leftarrow \{0,1\}^n \\ \text{Enc}_k(m_1m_2\dots m_n) &= c_1c_2\dots c_n \text{ where } c_i = m_i \oplus k_i \\  \text{Dec}_k(c_1c_2\dots c_n) &= m_1m_2\dots m_n \text{ where } m_i = c_i \oplus k_i  \end{aligned} $$

- One Time Pad is Perfectly Secret
- Key is as long as Message
